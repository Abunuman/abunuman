I am a **Registered Pharmacist (B.Pharm)** and **Biomedical Data Analyst** working at the intersection of **anti-infective drug discovery, wet-lab pharmacology, genomics & precision medicine and machine learning**.

### 🔬 Scientific & Technical Background
- **Wet-Lab Pharmacology & Microbiology:** Trained at the WHO-Accredited Ibadan National Poliovirus Laboratory (UCH Ibadan), executing BSL-2 mammalian cell culture, viral titration (TCID₅₀), cytotoxicity profiling (MTT), and natural-product fractionation.
- **Clinical & Pharmaceutical Perspective:** Experienced in hospital pharmacy, infectious-disease supply chains, and translating clinical trial evidence across anti-infective and CNS therapeutics.
- **Data Science & Computational Workflows:** Applying Python (`Pandas`, `NumPy`, `scikit-learn`), `SQL`, and statistical modeling to analyze biological screening data and health systems.

### 🎯 Research Interests
- Rational design and structure–activity relationship (SAR) optimization of novel antivirals and synthetic antibiotics
- Tackling antimicrobial resistance (AMR) and neuropathogenic viral infections
- Quantitative modeling and reproducible data pipelines for high-throughput bioassays
- Pharmacogenomics and Precision Medicine

### 🛠 Core Toolkit
- **Data & ML:** Python, SQL, scikit-learn, Pandas, Statistical Regression
- **Infrastructure & Reproducibility:** Linux/Bash, Git, Docker, AWS, Terraform, Jenkins, Github Action, Kubernetes
